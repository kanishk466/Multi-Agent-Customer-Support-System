# Refund & Billing Policy

## Refund Eligibility

Refunds are available for any purchase made within the last 30 days. Requests
made after 30 days are reviewed manually by the billing team and may be
approved as account credit instead of a cash refund. Monthly subscription fees
for the current billing period are not refundable once the period has started.

## Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Cards are
refressed in 5 to 7 business days. Bank transfers take 7 to 10 business days.
The customer receives an email with a refund reference number as soon as the
refund is issued.

## How To Request A Refund

The customer can request a refund from the dashboard under Billing, then
Billing History, then click Request Refund on the relevant invoice. If the
button is greyed out, the invoice is either older than 30 days or already
refunded. Alternatively the customer can reply to the billing receipt email
and the billing team will process it manually within one business day.

## Changing Or Cancelling A Plan

Plans can be upgraded at any time and the new price is prorated for the
remaining days of the cycle. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next
billing cycle. Cancelling a plan disables auto renewal but keeps the account
and data accessible until the end of the paid period.

## Failed Payments

A failed payment is retried automatically after 1, 3 and 5 days. After the
third failed retry the account is moved to a limited state where read access
is kept but new projects cannot be created. Updating the payment method and
clicking Retry Payment restores full access immediately.

## Invoices And GST

Invoices are generated on the 1st of each month and are downloadable from the
dashboard. GST or VAT numbers can be added under Billing, then Company Details
and appear on all future invoices. Invoices for past months can be reissued
with a corrected tax number by contacting billing, which takes 2 business days.

## Discounts And Credits

Annual plans receive a 20 percent discount compared to monthly billing.
Startup and nonprofit discounts of 30 percent are available on request with
valid documentation. Promotional credits never expire but cannot be exchanged
for cash.
