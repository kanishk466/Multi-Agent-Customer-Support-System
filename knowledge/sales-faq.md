# Sales And Pricing FAQ

## Pricing Plans

The Starter plan costs 19 dollars per month for 1 seat and 5 GB storage. The
Team plan costs 49 dollars per seat per month with 100 GB storage and
advanced permissions. Enterprise pricing is custom and starts at 500 dollars
per month with SSO, audit logs and a dedicated support contact.

## Trial Details

Every new account gets a 14 day trial of the Team plan with no credit card
required. At the end of the trial the account drops to the free tier and data
is kept for 90 days. A trial can be extended once by 7 days on request.

## Data Security

All data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES 256.
Backups run every hour and are retained for 30 days. The platform is SOC 2
Type II certified and a GDPR data processing agreement is available on
request. Data is stored in the region chosen at signup, either the EU or the
US.

## Migration From Another Tool

CSV and JSON imports are self service from the dashboard and cover the
majority of use cases. For customers coming from a competitor with more than
100 thousand records, the onboarding team runs a free assisted migration that
takes 3 to 5 business days. Historical data such as comments and attachments
is preserved where the source tool exports it.

## Cancellation Policy

Plans can be cancelled at any time from the dashboard with no cancellation
fee. Access continues until the end of the paid period. After that the
account moves to the free tier and data is retained for 90 days, so the
customer can reactivate without loss.

## Support Response Times

Team and Enterprise plans get email support with a first response within 4
business hours. Enterprise adds a shared chat channel with a 1 hour response
time. Starter plan support is email only with a first response within 2
business days. Support hours are Monday to Friday, 9 to 18 UTC.
